# lookdog-editorial-voice

Novamira skill, stored in WordPress (post 4513). This is a working copy for
review and version history; the live one is edited via `novamira/skill-edit`.

Triggers on: writing a guide, rewriting or humanizing an article, adding product
copy, varying an article's structure, or checking whether writing is on-brand.

The load-bearing section is **"Structure: make each article a different shape"**.
The six guides were written to one skeleton — corrective opening, three tables,
"The Short Version", italic disclaimer. Individually fine; six times over it is
the strongest artificial signal on the site, more than any sentence-level tell.
Sentence polish will not fix it and structural variety will.

Also encoded: never invent a quote (already broken once — the homepage pull quote
about chocolate was not in the feeding guide), never claim testing nobody did,
name what each category cannot do, and never place a product link in a passage
arguing against that product.
