## Layout

Source photos are all 4:3 landscape, so a straight 9:16 crop would have cut
Bell out of frame. Instead each photo sits full-width in a centre band with a
blurred, darkened copy of itself filling the space above and below. A slow Ken
Burns move runs on each photo, alternating zoom-in and zoom-out between slides.
30fps was chosen over 24 because slow moves on stills judder at 24.
