## Positions taken

- **Tug does not cause aggression.** The belief comes from dominance
  theory, abandoned by behaviour researchers decades ago. Saying so plainly
  is worth more than hedging.
- **Fetch is bad for joints in a specific way** — the hard, twisting
  deceleration, not the sprint. This matches the caveat already carried on
  the ball launcher product page, so the site does not contradict itself.
- **"Indestructible" means the manufacturer has not met your dog.** An
  affiliate site saying this about products it lists is the point.
- **Rope gets its own warning.** A swallowed strand acts as a linear
  foreign body, which is worse than a solid object of the same size.
