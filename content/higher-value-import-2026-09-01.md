# Higher-value import — 1 September 2026

## Why

The catalogue's median price was **$4.79**. At a few percent affiliate commission
that is roughly twenty cents a sale, which is not a business at any traffic
level. The brief was 20–30 products in the $25–80 band, where a commission is
worth collecting and where the buyer is exactly the person who reads a guide
first.

**The expensive end of AliExpress pet is where the quality collapses.** Cheap
items get bought casually and rated leniently; a $60 item that disappoints gets
a one-star review. Two-thirds to nine-tenths of everything above $25 sits below
the feedback floor this site has always applied.

Only two seams in that band are healthy: **water fountains** (15 candidates, most
of them marketed for cats) and **car seat covers** (16 candidates, nearly all the
same hammock). Both are near-duplicate clusters, and importing fifteen versions
of one fountain would break the rule the catalogue already follows.

The four fountains are four different answers (compact, standard, large, and
long-battery), not four versions of one. The four car products likewise: hammock,
walled bed, boot liner, footwell extender.

## The honesty each one carries

- Every car product says, in bold, that **it is not a restraint** — a hammock
  does nothing in an impact, and the dog still needs a tether or a crate.
- The fountains say they are **designed for cats**, and that a large dog will
  empty the small ones in a day.
- The grooming vacuum leads its Cons with **noise** — a dog that fears the hoover
  will not accept it at any setting, which is most of the one-star reviews in
  that category.
- The fleece bed says it is **filling, not foam**, and the wrong bed for an
  arthritic dog.
- The trolley case warns that **"extra large" on a carrier is not large by dog
  standards**, which is the mistake nearly every buyer makes.
- The rain coat states that **reflective is not the same as lit**.

## Effect on the catalogue

- 238 → **250 published products**
- Median price $4.79 → **$5.15**; average $9.51 → **$10.95**
- Products over $25: 20 → **32**
- Rating floor unchanged at **84.8%**

The median barely moved, and it will not until either more high-value products
clear the bar or some of the sub-$3 tail is retired. Worth a decision later.

## Verified

All 12 pages return 200 with a facts strip, a written Cons section and a tracked
buy button. All 12 affiliate links were followed to their landing page and
checked against the item they claim to be — AliExpress's two ID spaces differ by
exactly 2^51, and all twelve land where that arithmetic predicts. Sitemap rebuilt
and all 12 are in it.
